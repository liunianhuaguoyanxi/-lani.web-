Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 9, 2026

PIZZ AI ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data when you use our mobile application.

1. Data Collection & Privacy

No Personal Accounts: We do not require account registration or login. We do not collect personal identifiers such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Local Image Processing: When you use the "Analyze Screenshot" feature, the image is processed locally on your device using Apple's Vision framework. We do NOT upload, store, or share your photos on our servers.

AI Text Processing: Text extracted via OCR or pasted by the user is sent to our secure servers to generate AI replies. This data is processed in real-time and is not used for training AI models or stored permanently on our servers.

2. Subscription & Payments

All subscriptions and in-app purchases are handled securely by Apple's In-App Purchase system. We do not have access to your credit card or billing information.

3. Anonymous Analytics

We may collect anonymous technical data (e.g., app version, success rates of AI generation) solely to improve the app's performance and manage daily free quotas.

4. Data Retention

Since we do not store personal data on our servers, all your chat history and generated replies remain exclusively on your device. Deleting the app will remove all local data.
