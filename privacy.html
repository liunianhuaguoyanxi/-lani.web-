Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

Welcome to RIZZ AI. Your privacy is of the utmost importance to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Data Collection and Usage

Our app strictly respects your privacy through a principle of Data Minimization. We utilize Apple's native Vision framework to perform Optical Character Recognition (OCR) locally on your device.

We do not collect, upload, or store your screenshots, photos, or images. Only the text extracted from your chat or the text you manually input is collected and processed. This text is used exclusively to provide you with the core functionality of the App: generating AI reply suggestions.

2. Third-Party AI Data Sharing

To generate context-aware replies, the extracted text is securely transmitted to our third-party AI service provider, DeepSeek. We explicitly obtain your permission within the app via a prompt before any data is sent.

We confirm that DeepSeek, and any related entities with access to this data, provide the same or equal protection of user data as stated in this Privacy Policy and required by Apple's App Store Review Guidelines.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

The text data sent to our third-party AI provider is used strictly for real-time response generation. It is not retained, stored, or used to train AI models after the transaction is complete.

Because we do not store your data on our servers, there is no personal data for us to delete. However, you can easily revoke your consent for future data sharing at any time. To do this, navigate to the Settings menu within the App and select "Revoke AI Data Consent".

4. No Account Required

We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide personal identifiable information (such as your name, email, or phone number) to use the core features of RIZZ AI.

5. Security

We implement commercially reasonable security measures to protect the transmission of your text data. All communications between the App and our backend services, as well as third-party AI providers, are encrypted using standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top.
